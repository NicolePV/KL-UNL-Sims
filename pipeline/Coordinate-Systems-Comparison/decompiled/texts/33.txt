This simulator is designed to show how the rotation of the earth leads to the 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
apparent rotation of the sky and associated diurnal phenomena. In the upper left 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
panel the earth is shown at the center of the celestial sphere. The right panel 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
shows the corresponding horizon diagram for an observer at the indicated position 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
on the globe. As the earth rotates it seems to the observer that the sky is rotating, 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
causing stars to rise and set.

--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
 button. The 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
other way to add a star is to hold down the shift key and click on either sphere.

--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
and horizon systems to be displayed (these can also be edited). Click the star 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
again to deselect it.

To remove an individual star click on that star while holding down the delete key.
