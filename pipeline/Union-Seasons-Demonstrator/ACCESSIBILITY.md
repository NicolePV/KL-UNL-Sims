# Accessibility — Time-Lapse Seasons Demonstrator

Target: WCAG 2.1 AA, with AAA where it was reasonable.

**Human screen-reader QA is still required.** Everything below was verified by
inspecting the live DOM, computed styles and canvas pixels in a browser, and by
reasoning against how NVDA and VoiceOver behave. That is not a substitute for
someone actually listening to it on NVDA/Windows and VoiceOver/macOS and iOS.

## Structure

- One `<h1>`, rendered by the `<kl-unl-masthead>` component. The sim adds no
  competing `h1`.
- `<main>` wrapping four `<section class="panel">` elements, each labelled by its
  own `<h2>` via `aria-labelledby`. No heading levels are skipped.
- `<html lang="en">`.
- A skip link jumps past the diagram to the timeline controls.

## Text alternatives

| Graphic | Equivalent |
| --- | --- |
| The webcam photograph | `alt` rewritten on every day change: place, direction of view, full date, transit time with the time zone spelled out, and whether the day is overcast |
| The horizon-diagram canvas | `role="img"` with `aria-label`, plus `aria-describedby` pointing at a description regenerated by `render()`: the sun's altitude and declination with units, what the coloured circles mean, and the current viewing direction |
| The timeline canvas | `role="img"` with a label describing the year scale and the no-image bands |
| The "show directions" overlay, the legend swatch | decorative, `alt=""` / `aria-hidden` |

The diagram description is rebuilt from the same `state` as the pixels, so it
cannot drift out of sync with what is on screen.

## Units are always spoken

This was an explicit requirement, and the failure it guards against — values read
as bare numbers — is real. Every number that carries a unit is announced with
both the quantity name and the unit:

- `aria-valuetext` on the sun handle: *"Sun's declination minus 6.3 degrees, on
  Thursday, October 9, 2003."*
- `aria-valuetext` on the timeline cursor: *"Thursday, October 9, 2003, day 283
  of 366."*
- `aria-valuetext` on the rate slider: *"Animation rate 30 days per second."*
  (the underlying quantity is days per millisecond, which does not speak well)
- `.sr-only` companions to the two readouts: *"Sun's altitude 42.9 degrees."*,
  *"Declination minus 6.3 degrees."*
- Negative values are spoken as **"minus 6.3 degrees"**, never "−6.3", which both
  NVDA and VoiceOver are liable to drop or read as a dash.
- Time zones are expanded: "CDT" → "Central Daylight Time".

## Live region

A single `aria-live="polite"` `.sr-only` region announces committed changes:
day changes, view rotations, checkbox toggles, rate changes, animation start and
pause, and reset. Announcements fire **on commit** — on pointer release, on a key
press, on a `change` event — never on every drag move or animation tick, so the
region does not flood. A short debounce coalesces rapid changes.

## Keyboard

Everything is operable from the keyboard, and the tab order contains **only**
interactive controls — verified: no readout, label, log line, canvas, or MathJax
container is a tab stop.

| Control | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Timeline cursor (`role="slider"`) | ←/↓ back a day, →/↑ forward a day, PageUp/PageDown ±30 days, Home first day, End last day, mouse wheel ±1 day while focused |
| Sun disc (`role="slider"`) | ←/↓ and →/↑ step a day, PageUp/PageDown ±7 days, Home summer solstice, End winter solstice, mouse wheel ±1 day while focused |
| Rotate view | ←/→ azimuth ±5° (±15° with Shift), ↑/↓ elevation, PageUp/PageDown ±15°, Home restores the original view |
| Rate slider | native `<input type="range">` — arrows, PageUp/PageDown, Home/End all free; a wheel handler adds wheel adjustment while focused |
| Buttons, checkboxes | native activation |

Both draggable objects satisfy the two required focus behaviours: they are
reachable by **Tab**, and **clicking or tapping them also focuses them** (the
handler calls `.focus()` on `pointerdown`), so the arrow keys work immediately
after a click without tabbing first. Focus rings come from the foundation's
`:focus-visible`. Tab always moves away normally — there are no traps. The
masthead dialog manages its own focus and Escape; the sim does not interfere.

Pointer and keyboard paths mutate the same `state` object and go through the
same `render()`, so they cannot disagree.

## MathJax

All mathematics — the latitude/longitude line and both angle readouts — is
typeset by MathJax from LaTeX through the foundation's `klunlShowEquation()`,
paired with a spoken `.sr-only` description. Nothing mathematical is a raster
image, ASCII art, hand-built `<sup>`, or canvas-painted text. Right-clicking any
of it opens MathJax's own context menu; that menu is not disabled or overridden.

MathJax v3 puts `tabindex="0"` on every `mjx-container`, which would put
display-only output into the tab order. A `MutationObserver` demotes each
container to `tabindex="-1"` as it appears. The right-click menu still works, and
the maths is still read aloud through the paired descriptions. Verified live: 0
containers at `tabindex="0"`, 3 at `tabindex="-1"`.

## Colour and contrast

Colour is never the only carrier of meaning: every state the diagram encodes in
colour is also stated numerically in the readouts and in the diagram description.

**One colour was remapped.** The cardinal-direction labels were white text
painted straight onto the green horizon plane, which measures **3.17:1** against
`#3aa53a` — short of the 4.5:1 WCAG 1.4.3 asks of text. The glyphs stay white, so
they still read as the original did, but now sit on a `rgba(0,0,0,0.55)` chip,
raising them to roughly **9.8:1** and keeping them legible over the sky when the
view is rotated.

**Two original colours were kept.** The yellow sun path (`#ffcc00` at 70% alpha)
and the blue celestial equator (`#2c7bfe` at 60%) are physically meaningful and
match the original. Against the pale sky the yellow path is a weak graphical
contrast. It was left alone deliberately — the prompt's guidance is to keep
meaningful colour and supplement it, which is done: the sun's declination is
given as a number in the readouts, in the sun handle's `aria-valuetext`, and in
the diagram description, and the two white limit circles are named in the
description as the solstice limits. **Worth a sighted-reviewer look** if this
panel is ever shown on a projector or in bright ambient light.

Body text is `#1a1a1a` on `#ffffff` (16.9:1). All palette values come from the
foundation's CSS custom properties.

## Type size, zoom and reflow

- Body copy is `1.125rem` — above the required floor and well above the
  foundation's own `0.9rem` default, which `styles/styles.css` overrides without
  editing the foundation.
- Everything is sized in `rem`/`em`, so it tracks the browser font setting.
- No fixed pixel heights crop text. The canvases keep their original internal
  coordinate systems and are scaled by CSS with a preserved aspect ratio, so the
  drawing and physics maths never sees the on-screen size and 200% zoom reflows
  rather than clipping.
- Verified at 1280, 900, 760, 414, 360 and 320px: **no horizontal scrolling at
  any width**, the two columns collapse to a single stacked column below the
  foundation's 56rem breakpoint, and while side by side their heights stay
  equal. Reaching this at 320px required containing an overflow that originates
  inside the masthead component itself — see CONVERSION_NOTES.md, foundation
  bug 2.
- Canvas-internal text was moved into HTML wherever it existed — the month names
  and the N/E/S/W labels — so it zooms with the page. **No text remains baked
  into either canvas.**

## Motion

- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` suppresses the continuous animation; the live
  region says so and points at the timeline and arrow keys. Every day stays
  reachable by every other route.
- The animation has an explicit **pause** — the same button toggles between
  "start animation" and "pause animation", exactly as the original did. Reset
  comes from the masthead's `sim-reset` event; the sim adds no second Reset.
- Nothing flashes. The animation redraws a photograph and a diagram at frame
  rate; there is no flashing above 3 Hz at any rate setting.

## Touch and pointer

- Pointer Events throughout, so mouse and touch share one path; this works on iOS
  Safari.
- `touch-action: none` on the sphere, the sun handle, and the timeline, so
  dragging does not scroll or zoom the page. Normal scrolling is untouched
  elsewhere.
- Pointer coordinates are mapped back through the live scale factor into the
  original stage units, so drag and snapping match the ActionScript at any size.
- Targets are at least 44×44 px: buttons 44 px tall, the sun handle 44×44, the
  timeline cursor 44×44 (it keeps its own minimum and overhangs the strip rather
  than shrinking with it on a phone), and the checkbox labels are stretched to a
  44 px minimum around the foundation's 20 px box — without forking the
  foundation's look.
- Nothing is hover-only.

## Forms

Every input has a real `<label>` — verified live: zero inputs without an
accessible name.

## Known gaps

- No human screen-reader pass has been done. See the note at the top.
- The yellow sun path's graphical contrast is discussed above and was a
  deliberate keep, not an oversight.
- Rotating the view has no on-screen affordance hinting that the sphere is
  draggable; this matches the original, which also relied on the Help text.
