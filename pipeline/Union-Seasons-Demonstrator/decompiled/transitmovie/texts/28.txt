This file demonstrates seasonal changes through a sequence of images taken over the course of a year. Each of the images was taken when the sun reached its highest point in the sky (the meridional transit). The camera was pointed east, so the direction of the sun is up and to the right.The horizon diagram in the upper right panel represents the sky for an observer in the picture. As the day of year changes the sun's daily path (the yellow circle) moves up and down between the two white declination circles. These limits correspond to the winter and summer solstices. Note that the sun's disc is draggable in the diagram.The timeline in the bottom panel can also be used to control which day's image is shown. Below the timeline are dark bands that indicate which days do not have an image associated with them. There is an option to exclude overcast days, which are days when the building does not cast a shadow. This can make the movement of the shadow more apparent when animating.About the images: These images were captured from the University of Nebraska-Lincoln's webcam in 2003 and 2004. The scene is Memorial Plaza north of the Student Union.
