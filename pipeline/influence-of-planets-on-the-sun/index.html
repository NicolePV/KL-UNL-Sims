Animation speed controls
Type a whole number of days per second from 0 to 5000 and press Enter.

The Sun is drawn to scale; its motion around the center of mass is not to scale relative to the planets’ orbits, which lie far outside the picture. Keyboard: the speed slider responds to the left and right arrow keys (1 day per second), Page Up and Page Down (larger steps) and Home and End (0 and 5000).
