Celestial Sphere

NCP SCP CE CE NP SP

Drag the observer's horizon line in the diagram to change the latitude, or use the arrow keys once it is focused. Left and Down arrow decrease the latitude by one degree and Right and Up arrow increase it by one degree. Page Down and Page Up change it by ten degrees. Home and End jump to 90 degrees south and 90 degrees north.

Observer's Latitude

Type a latitude in degrees, from minus 90 for the south pole to 90 for the north pole. Negative values are south of the equator. Use the up and down arrow keys, or the mouse wheel while the field is focused, to change the value by a tenth of a degree; Page Up and Page Down change it by ten degrees.
