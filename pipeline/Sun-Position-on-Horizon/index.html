Keyboard controls
  • Tab moves between the view choices, the date slider, the month and day fields, and the Play button.
  • On the date slider, Left/Down and Right/Up change the date by one day, Page Up/Page Down by ten days, and Home/End jump to the ends of the year. The mouse wheel also changes the date while the slider is focused.
  • In the Day field, type a number, or use Up/Down, Page Up/Page Down, Home/End, or the mouse wheel. In the Month field, use Up/Down or type the first letter of a month.
  • Space or Enter starts and stops the animation.
  • Reset in the title bar returns the simulation to March 21, sunrise, paused.
