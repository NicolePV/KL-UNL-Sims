Days From Peak

Left and Right arrow keys shift the observations in time by one day (hold Shift for ten days; Home and End jump to the smallest and largest time shift). Up and Down arrow keys move the observations vertically by 0.1 magnitudes of distance modulus (hold Shift, or use Page Up and Page Down, for 1 magnitude). The vertical shift is the distance modulus, apparent minus absolute magnitude.

Distance Modulus Calculator

The distance modulus formula: apparent magnitude m minus absolute magnitude M equals negative 5 plus 5 times the base-10 logarithm of the distance d in parsecs.
