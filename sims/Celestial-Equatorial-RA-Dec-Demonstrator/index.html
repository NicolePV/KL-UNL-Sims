Keyboard Navigation

Drag and use the arrow keys (or A, D, W, and S) to rotate the view, amplifying the effect with the Shift or Caps Lock keys. Click on the star and use the same keys to move it in right ascension and declination. Toggle label visibility with the 1 (North and South Poles), 2 (Equator), 3 (North and South Celestial Poles), 4 (Celestial Equator), 5 (0h circle), 6 (East Arrow), and 7 (Ecliptic) keys. Reset the simulation with R.
