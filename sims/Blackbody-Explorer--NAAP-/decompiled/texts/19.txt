This explorer demonstrates how the blackbody spectrum – that is, the energy emitted by a thermal source – varies with temperature. Stars, to a reasonable first approximation, can be considered as blackbodies.The left panel shows the plot of intensity vs. wavelength (the spectrum) for one or more blackbodies. The rainbow indicates the visible part of the electromagnetic spectrum.The curves are controlled by the settings in the curves panel at right. It is possible to add several curves, but only one curve can be selected at a time. The panels in the lower right control the scaling of the plot window.The filters panel lets one see how a star with the same distribution as the selected curve would look through various filters commonly used by astronomers.
