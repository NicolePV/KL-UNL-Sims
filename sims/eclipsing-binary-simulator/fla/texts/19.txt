In practice eclipsing binary stars are so close together that astronomers see just a 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
single combined 'star'. The plot in the upper right shows the combined brightness of the 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
system. Dips in the lightcurve correspond to the eclipses as seen from earth.

The panel in the lower right controls the properties of the stars. Note that as you adjust 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
one slider the range of the other sliders may change (indicated by the dark shading) to 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
prevent the stars from physically overlapping. The longitude and inclination properties 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
in the left panel control the orientation of the system with respect to earth.
