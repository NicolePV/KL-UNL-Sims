Keyboard Navigation

Drag and use the arrow keys (or A, D, W, and S) to rotate the view, amplifying the effect with the Shift or Caps Lock keys. Click on the star and use the same keys to move it in azimuth and altitude. Toggle label visibility with the 1 (zenith), 2 (nadir), 3 (horizon), and 4 (meridian) keys. Reset the simulation with R.
