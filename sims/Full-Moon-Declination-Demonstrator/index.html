Keyboard Navigation

On the horizon diagram, drag or use the arrow keys (or A, D, W, and S) to rotate the view. On the declination plot, drag or use the same keys to change the day of year. Increase or decrease the latitude similarly with the plus and minus keys. Amplify steps with Shift or Caps Lock, and reset the simulation with R.
