Big Dipper Diagram

Drag the constellation, or use arrow keys, to rotate the view. Users may also use the A and D keys to rotate the view left and right, and the W and S keys to rotate the viewing angle up and down.

Keyboard Navigation

Drag and use the arrow keys (or A, D, W, and S) to rotate the view, amplifying the effect with the Shift or Caps Lock keys. Toggle sightline visibility with the L key, and reset the simulation with R.
