# KL-UNL Simulations

## Blink Comparator Simulator
This simulator cycles through multiple images of the same field, revealing
variable stars. The variable stars identified in this simulator can then be
analyzed with the variable star Photometry Analyzer.

## Center of Mass Simulator
This simulator shows how the center of mass of two objects changes as their
masses change.

## Circumstellar-habitable-zone-simulator
This simulator demonstrates the location and evolution of the stellar
habitable zone, the region around a star where surface water may exist on an
earth-like planet.

## Eclipsing-binary-simulator
This simulator models the motions of two stars in orbit around each
other. When such a system is aligned properly the stars will eclipse one
another, causing a change in brightness (as seen from earth) that allows
astronomers to determine the properties of the stars.

## Exoplanet-transit-simulator
This simulator shows what happens when an extrasolar planet passes in front of
its star. Such eclipses (called transits) allow scientists to detect planets
they would not see otherwise.

## Galactic-redshift-simulator
This simulator demonstrates the redshift of a galaxy due to the expansion of
the universe, and the effect this shift has on the galaxy's brightness as
observed through various filters.

## Gas-retention-plot
This interactive plot shows how molecular mass, temperature and escape speed
determine whether a gas will remain gravitationally bound to a
planet.

## Gas-retention-simulator
This simulator demonstrates how gases of different molecular masses behave
when maintained at thermodynamic equilibrium in a chamber. The chamber can be
set to allow particles that exceed a certain speed to escape, providing an
analogy for the bleeding off of a planet’s atmosphere into space.

## HR Diagram Cluster Fitting Explorer
This explorer allows one to determine the distance to a cluster by fitting the
cluster's stars to the main sequence in an HR diagram.

## Influence-of-planets-on-the-sun
This explorer shows the movement of the sun due to the gravitational pull of
the planets. The contribution from each planet can be isolated by toggling
checkboxes.

## Milky-way-habitability-explorer
This explorer shows how two factors important to life &ndash; metallicity and
extinction risk &ndash; vary throughout the Milky Way Galaxy.

## Milky-way-rotational-velocity-explorer
This explorer shows a plot of the rotational velocity of stars at varying
distances from the center of the milky way. A draggable cursor allows
determining the contained mass implied by the curve.

## Parallax-explorer
This explorer demonstrates the concept of parallax and how it can be used to
determine distance using an analogy of a boat on a lake.

## Photometry-simulator
This simulator demonstrates aperture photometry by allowing one to drag
aperture discs over a star field.

## Ptolemaic-system-simulator
This simulator demonstrates how Ptolemy’s geocentric model accounts for the
movements of the planets. In this model the sun, moon, and planets orbit the
stationary earth. A planet’s path is described by a circle-on-circle
construction: a planet moves on a circle called the <em>epicycle</em> which in
turn moves on a larger circle called the <em>deferent</em>.

## Pulsar-period-simulator
This simulator demonstrates how the movement of a pulsar and planet around
their common center of mass affects the timing of pulse arrivals.

## Radial-velocity-simulator
This simulator models the motion of an extrasolar planet and its star around
their common center of mass. Although the star does not move very much
scientists can use the doppler shift of the star’s absorption lines to
determine its speed with great accuracy. This allows them to infer the
existence and properties of a planet they can not see directly.

## Registration-simulator
This simulator allows one to adjust the relative positions of three images so
that their contents are aligned.

## Supernova-light-curve-fitting-explorer
This explorer allows one to determine the distance to a supernova by fitting
observations to a theoretical Type Ia light curve.

## Variable-star-photometry-analyzer
This analyzer demonstrates variable star analysis through period determination
and light curve folding.
