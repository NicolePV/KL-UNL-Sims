This graph shows the hours of daylight received during the year for an observer at a given latitude. This is an important factor contributing to the seasons.Use the latitude and day of year sliders in the Settings panel to make changes to the plot. The globe shows where the Sun's light is directed on the specified day. You can drag the globe to change the perspective.The precise dates of the equinoxes and solstices vary from year to year. For the next few decades the most common dates will be March 20,June 21, September 22, and December 21.
