Controls

Move the Earth or Moon disc by dragging it, or click a disc (or press Tab to reach it) to focus it and then use the arrow keys — hold Shift for larger steps. You can also use the sliders below. The shadow each disc casts away from the Sun updates automatically; the Sun stays fixed at the left.
