Horizon Diagram

Interactive horizon diagram. Drag the sphere, or use the arrow keys, to rotate the view. Drag the sun to move it through the year, or press Enter to switch the arrow keys to moving the sun (Page Up and Page Down jump by a week; Home and End jump to the ends of the year). Press Enter again to return to rotating the view. The latitude slider and the animate control are in the Settings panel.
