The Horizon Diagram

insolation: 0

Interactive horizon diagram. Drag the celestial sphere, or use arrow keys, to rotate the view. Users may also use the A and D keys to rotate the view left and right, and the W and S keys to rotate the viewing angle up and down. Press Enter for arrow keys to move the sun (Page Up and Page Down jump by a week; Home jumps to Spring Equinox). Press Enter once more to change observer's latitude (arrow keys move one degree; Page Up and Page Down move ten degrees). Press Enter again to return to rotating viewpoint. A date slider, latitude slider, and animate and insolation controls are in the Settings panel.

Keyboard Navigation

Drag and use the arrow keys (or A, D, W, and S) to rotate the view, amplifying the effect with the Shift or Caps Lock keys. Press enter once to update the date similarly, and a second time to update the observer's latitude. Play or pause the animation with P, toggle insolation with I, and reset the simulation with R.
