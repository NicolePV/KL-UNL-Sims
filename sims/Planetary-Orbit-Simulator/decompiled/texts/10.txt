according to Kepler's laws of motion.

--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
Orbit Settings
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
 panel. Presets for the existing planets can be loaded by 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
selecting a planet from the combo box and clicking OK.

Since the planets take months to years to orbit the sun the animation is

shown speeded up. You can control the animation rate in the 
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
Animation

Controls
--- RECORDSEPARATOR ---
 panel. The rate is specified in simulated years per second.
