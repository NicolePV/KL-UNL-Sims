Each grid square is one distance unit on a side, so the grid rescales when you change the separation. The blue arrow spans the distance from object 1 to the center of mass; the red arrow spans the distance from object 2 to the center of mass.

Object Masses and Separation

Mass 1 number box and slider controls

Mass 2 number box and slider controls

Object separation number box and slider controls

Masses are in mass units, from 1.0 to 10.0 in steps of 0.1. Type a value and press Enter, or use the arrow keys on the slider.

Separation is in distance units, from 1.0 to 20.0 in steps of 0.1. Type a value and press Enter, or use the arrow keys on the slider.

When checked, the center of mass stays in one place and the two objects shift back and forth to its right and left.
