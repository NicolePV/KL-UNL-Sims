Each grid square is one distance unit on a side, so the grid rescales when you change the separation. The blue arrow spans the distance from object 1 to the center of mass; the red arrow spans the distance from object 2 to the center of mass.

Controls

Object Masses and Separation

Mass 1 number box and slider controls

Mass 2 number box and slider controls

Object separation number box and slider controls

Masses are in mass units, from 1.0 to 10.0 in steps of 0.1. Type a value and press Enter, or use the up and down arrow keys; hold Shift for steps of 1.

Separation is in distance units, from 1.0 to 20.0 in steps of 0.1. Type a value and press Enter, or use the up and down arrow keys; hold Shift for steps of 1.

When checked, the center of mass stays in one place and the two objects shift back and forth to its right and left.

Keyboard Navigation

Use the arrow keys (or A and D) to change the mass of Object 1, amplifying the effect with the Shift or Caps Lock keys. Press enter once to update the mass of Object 2 similarly, and a second time to update the object separation. Keep the center of mass fixed or free with F, and reset the simulation with R.
