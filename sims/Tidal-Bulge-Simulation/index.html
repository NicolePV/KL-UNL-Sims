Simulation options

The Earth (center) is wrapped by its blue tidal bulges, which point along the Earth–Moon line. Turn on Run to set the Moon orbiting. Include Sun adds the day/night shading and makes the bulges grow and shrink between spring and neap tides. Include Effects of Earth's Rotation drags the bulges ahead of the Moon.
