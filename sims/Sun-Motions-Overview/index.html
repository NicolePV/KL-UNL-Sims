Celestial sphere

Celestial sphere surrounding horizon plane. Drag with the pointer, or use the left and right arrow keys to rotate the view and the up and down arrow keys to change the viewing angle. Users may also use the A and D keys to rotate the view left and right, and the W and S keys to rotate the viewing angle up and down.
