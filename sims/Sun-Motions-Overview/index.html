Celestial Sphere

Celestial sphere surrounding horizon plane. Drag with the pointer, or use the left and right arrow keys to rotate the view and the up and down arrow keys to change the viewing angle. Users may also use the A and D keys to rotate the view left and right, and the W and S keys to rotate the viewing angle up and down.

Keyboard Navigation

Drag and use the arrow keys (or A, D, W, and S) to rotate the view, amplifying the effect with the Shift or Caps Lock keys. Adjust observer latitude similarly with the plus and minus keys. Toggle arc and line visibility with the 1 (poles), 2 (celestial equator), 3 (equinox path), and 4 (solstice path) keys. Reset the simulation with R.
