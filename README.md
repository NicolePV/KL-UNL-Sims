# KL-UNL-Sims

Modernization of Kevin Lee's University of Nebraska-Lincoln 
astronomy education simulations

Legacy Flash simulations have been rewritten in HTML5 with 
supporting Javascript and CSS, and updated to support WCAG 
AA accessibility guidelines. A common foundation provides a 
unified style and UX experience.

Simulations remain available online at 
[Astronomy Education at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln](https://astro.unl.edu/animationsLinks.html) 
and are mirrored at 
[New Mexico State University](http://astronomy.nmsu.edu/geas/klunl/sims/).

Simulations are in the process of being rewritten at present.
